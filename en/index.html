FAQ

Before you start

How do I cancel?

In the customer portal — the link is in every report email. You sign in with the email address the reports are sent to. The subscription runs until the end of the paid period and nothing more is charged.

I don’t know who my competitors are.

No problem. We derive your field from your website and find three companies offering comparable products or services. If we get it wrong, tell us and we will leave them out next time.

Is the legal check a replacement for a lawyer?

No. It is an automated check of the publicly available page against twelve points of EU consumer and data protection law. It warns you about risks early; for serious findings we recommend consulting a lawyer.

I sell outside the EU. Does the legal check apply to me?

The monthly check follows EU law. If you sell elsewhere, reply to any report email and we will check your website against the rules of your country as part of your subscription.

What exactly do you check?

The homepage of the address you enter. Information shown only on other pages (terms, contact) is not taken into account.

Will I get an invoice?

Yes, a receipt arrives by email after every payment and is also available in the customer portal.
