2. The service

Shop Watch is a subscription in which the provider delivers automated analytical reports about the website address entered by the customer, as interactive HTML reports sent by email:

  • Competitor analysis every week — comparison of the customer’s homepage with up to three competitors (found by the provider or entered by the customer) and changes since the previous measurement.
  • Full Store Review every month — a CRO audit of the homepage (conversion barriers, proposed fixes, a roadmap) and an automated check against twelve points of EU consumer and data protection law.
  • If the customer sells outside the EU, the provider will check the website against the rules of the customer’s country on request, as part of the subscription.

Nature of the output. All reports are generated automatically, including with the use of artificial intelligence tools. Recommendations are for information only and do not guarantee any business results. The legal check is an automated technical check of publicly available content, not legal advice or a legal service. Only the page at the entered address is checked, not the whole website. The provider does not guarantee the accuracy or completeness of the findings and recommends consulting a lawyer about serious findings.
